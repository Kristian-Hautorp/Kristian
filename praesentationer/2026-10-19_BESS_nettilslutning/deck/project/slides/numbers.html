The table shows the change per project. For the three projects that had an earlier battery design (Bukholt 2, Gestengen and Brønderslev), power drops by about a quarter and stored energy by about half. For Voergaard there is no earlier design figure to compare with; it is shown with the new two-hour design. Across the portfolio the new design totals about 354 MW and 708 MWh. Bukholt 2 and Gestengen connect to the national transmission grid (Energinet) through a shared 150 kV connection; Brønderslev and Voergaard connect to the local grid company, Nord Energi, at 60 kV. All figures are draft planning figures and remain subject to final design and grid operator approval.
