The table shows the change per project. For Brønderslev, power drops from 90 to 60 MW while storage rises from 180 to 240 MWh. For Voergaard, power drops from 56.25 to 45 MW while storage rises from 112.5 to 180 MWh. Together that is about 28% less power and 44% more stored energy. Both revised applications were filed with the local grid company, Nord Energi Net, on 17 September 2026 and accept limited grid access; the response is outstanding. Bukholt 2 and Gestengen connect to the national transmission grid through Energinet; the revised figures for these two will be added. Until the grid operators approve the new designs, the previous figures remain the contracted ones.
