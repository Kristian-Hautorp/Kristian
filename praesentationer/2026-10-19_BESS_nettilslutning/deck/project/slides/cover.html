Right-sized batteries for a faster grid connection

Why we changed the size and power of our Danish battery storage (BESS), and why we believe it is the right call

Stern Energy for Encavis · October 2026 · Draft for discussion

Purpose of this deck: explain to investors, in plain terms, why we redesigned the batteries planned next to our four Danish solar parks: lower power (MW), longer storage (4 hours instead of 2) and limited grid access, and why we believe this gives us the fastest realistic route to a grid connection. Storyline: 1) Denmark needs much more green power and storage. 2) But the grid is full, and in 2026 Denmark introduced new rules on who gets connected first. 3) Under these rules, grid-friendly projects move up the queue. 4) We redesigned our batteries to be grid-friendly: less power, more hours, limited grid access, sized to serve the solar park.
