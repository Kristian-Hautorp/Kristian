Project team assessment against the political agreement of 1 October 2026; final classification rests with the grid operators

11

Four reasons we believe the new design improves our chances. One: battery power is cut to 50 to 60% of the solar connection, so the battery puts less load on the grid when it charges and discharges. Two: we accept limited grid access, meaning the grid operator may curtail the battery when the grid is congested. That is the first thing grid operators look at when they judge grid-friendliness. Three: four hours of storage lets the battery absorb more of the midday solar peak and deliver it over the evening, through the connection the solar park already has. Four: because the battery is built around our own solar output, we have a stronger argument that it is not stand-alone storage ‘beyond ordinary production needs’, which is how the law defines category 3. To be clear: the final classification is made by Energinet and Nord Energi Net, case by case. We are positioning our projects as strongly as we can within the new rules.
