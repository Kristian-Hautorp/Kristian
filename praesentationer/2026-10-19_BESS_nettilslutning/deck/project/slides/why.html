11

Four reasons we believe the new design improves our chances. One: the battery is designed to stay within the connection the solar park already has, so we use existing grid capacity better instead of asking for more. Two: because it is built around our own solar output, we have a stronger argument that it is not stand-alone storage ‘beyond ordinary production needs’, which is how the law defines category 3. Three: lower charging power means we add less new demand to a queue that is mainly a demand queue, and the battery can charge from our own solar when the grid is tight. Four: a battery next to solar is better placed to accept limited or interruptible access, which is the first thing grid operators look at when they judge grid-friendliness. To be clear: the final classification is made by Energinet and Nord Energi, case by case. We are positioning our projects as strongly as we can within the new rules.
