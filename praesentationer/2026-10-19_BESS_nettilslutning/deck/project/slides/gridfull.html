In March 2026 Energinet, the Danish transmission system operator, reported that about 60 GW of new electricity demand was queuing for a connection across the transmission and distribution grids. For comparison, Denmark’s maximum electricity consumption is about 7 GW. Energinet named data centres, batteries and Power-to-X plants as the main drivers. A battery counts as demand when it charges, so large batteries add to this queue. That is why the design of a battery now decides whether, and when, it gets connected.
