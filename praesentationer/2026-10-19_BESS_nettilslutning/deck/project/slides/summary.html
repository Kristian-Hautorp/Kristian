In one sentence

Right-sized batteries next to our solar parks are our fastest realistic route onto the Danish grid.

1 · The need

Denmark needs more green power and storage, but the grid is full.

2 · The rules

New rules put grid-friendly projects first within each category.

3 · Our answer

We cut battery power by about 25% and energy by about 50% to be one of them.

If investors remember one thing: we did not shrink the batteries because we believe less in storage. We resized them because, under Denmark’s new grid rules, a battery that is sized to serve its solar park and behaves in a grid-friendly way has a much better chance of getting a connection, and getting it sooner. Denmark needs both more green power and more storage, the grid is the bottleneck, and the new rules reward projects that make better use of the grid that already exists. Our redesign puts our four projects on the right side of those rules.
