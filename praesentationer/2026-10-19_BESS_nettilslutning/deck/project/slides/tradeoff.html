Our choice

The trade-off we accept

What we give up

Less energy to trade each day: 2 hours of storage instead of about 3

Lower maximum battery output per project

What we gain

A stronger, grid-friendly application under the new rules

A better chance of an earlier grid connection

A smaller up-front battery investment per project

Battery revenue can start sooner if the connection comes sooner

Our view: a battery that gets connected is worth more than a bigger battery waiting in the queue.

Qualitative assessment by the project team. Financial impact to be quantified separately.

12

We want to be transparent about the trade-off. A smaller battery stores less energy each day and has a lower maximum output, so the revenue potential per project is lower than in the earlier design. In return we get an application that fits the new rules much better, a better chance of being connected earlier, and a smaller up-front investment per project. In a market where grid access is the scarce resource, time to connection matters more than size. A battery that is connected and earning is worth more than a larger one that waits years in the queue. The financial impact is being quantified separately; this slide is a qualitative assessment.
