Sources: Political agreement on the grid emergency plan, 1 Oct 2026, pp. 2 and 6; Danish Climate Ministry news, 1 Oct 2026

05

2026 changed everything for grid access in Denmark. In March, Energinet paused new large connection agreements for three months because demand had outgrown capacity. In June it restarted with a new model: large customers are handled in pools and judged on maturity, progress and grid-friendliness instead of first-come, first-served. On 1 October 2026 Parliament adopted the political ‘emergency plan for the grid’, which gives Energinet and the local grid companies a legal basis to prioritise by category. The rules took effect in October, just before Energinet’s next allocation of large connection agreements this autumn. Politicians will review the model by the end of 2027 and then every two years, so the rules may still evolve.
