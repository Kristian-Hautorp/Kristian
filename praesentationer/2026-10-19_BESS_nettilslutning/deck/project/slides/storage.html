Batteries help, if they are grid-friendly

“Energy storage in district heating and batteries can play an important role in using the full capacity of the grid.”

Danish political agreement on the grid emergency plan, 1 October 2026 (our translation)

The same agreement adds a condition: storage can help balance the power system and use the grid better, but it can also burden the grid if it does not act grid-friendly.

03

Denmark’s politicians explicitly see a role for batteries: they can help use the full capacity of the grid. But the same agreement adds a clear condition: storage helps only if it behaves in a grid-friendly way. A battery that charges at the wrong time or in the wrong place adds to the problem. This is the core of our story: the question is not whether Denmark wants batteries, but which batteries it wants. Our answer is batteries that work together with solar and are sized so they relieve the grid rather than load it.
