The Danish government aims to quadruple electricity production from solar and onshore wind by 2030. In numbers: 12 TWh in 2021, about 25 TWh expected in 2030 without new policy, and about 50 TWh with the target. That is roughly double what would happen anyway. The reason is simple: green power must replace fossil fuels in transport, heating and industry. Key point for investors: our four solar parks are exactly the kind of project Denmark says it needs.
