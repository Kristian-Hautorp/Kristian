Our four projects sit in two clusters. In West Jutland, Bukholt 2 and Gestengen share one 150 kV connection to the national transmission grid at Lem Kær, run by Energinet. In North Jutland, Brønderslev and Voergaard connect to the local grid company, Nord Energi, at 60 kV. The new priority rules apply to both Energinet and the local grid companies, so all four batteries are assessed under the same national categories and grid-friendliness criteria. In each case the battery is planned next to the solar park and designed to work through the solar park’s connection. The battery figures shown are the new four-hour designs: Brønderslev 60 MW / 240 MWh and Voergaard 45 MW / 180 MWh, filed with Nord Energi Net on 17 September 2026. The new figures for Bukholt 2 and Gestengen will be added.
