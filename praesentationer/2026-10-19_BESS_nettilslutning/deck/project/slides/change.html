Here is the change in one picture. The dashed bar is the solar park’s grid connection. Each block is one hour of storage, and the width of a block is the battery’s power. In the previous design the battery had about 75% of the solar connection’s power and two hours of storage, with full grid access. In the new design the battery’s power is cut to 50 to 60% of the solar connection, storage is doubled to four hours, and we accept limited grid access, meaning the grid operator may curtail the battery when the grid is congested. For Brønderslev and Voergaard together, that is about 28% less power and about 44% more stored energy. The revised applications for these two projects were filed with Nord Energi Net on 17 September 2026; the response is outstanding. The figures for Bukholt 2 and Gestengen will follow.
