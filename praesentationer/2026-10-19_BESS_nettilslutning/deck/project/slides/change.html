Here is the change in one picture. Each block is one hour of storage; the width of a block is the battery’s power. In the earlier design, each battery had roughly the same power as the solar park’s output and about three hours of storage. In the new design, battery power is about 75% of the solar park’s output and storage is two hours. Across the three projects that had an earlier design, that means about 25% less power and about 50% less energy. The battery is now sized to work with the solar park, not to use the grid connection on its own. The figures are draft planning figures and subject to final design.
